# Pricing and Licensing Direction

## Recommended model
- Open-source core under Apache 2.0 or MIT
- Commercial add-ons for support, enterprise deployment, and advanced observability

## Suggested offers
- Community: free, self-hosted, community support
- Pro: paid support and deployment guidance for small teams
- Enterprise: SLA-backed deployment, security review, and private support

## Why this works
This keeps the project accessible while creating a clear paid path for teams that need reliability and support rather than just code.
